FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Download the app and use every feature free for 30 days, with no card and no sign-up. Pick a plan when you're ready, and your products and bills stay as they are.

Do I need internet to bill?

No. Every sale is saved on the device first. Internet only matters for cloud sync, which catches up by itself once you're back online. Backups and updates work from a pen drive.

What if my phone or PC breaks?

Back up from Settings → Backup to a pen drive (a PC can do it every day by itself). On the new device choose Restore from backup, pick the file and your shop's licence: products, customers, bills and staff come back. The file is encrypted and opens only with your licence.

What happens when my plan ends?

You get 30 days of grace with a reminder banner. After that the app goes read-only: you can still see every bill, report and customer, but new sales stop until you renew. Your data is never locked away or deleted.

How is the price counted?

Per device that sells or manages the shop: a phone, tablet or PC. Two counters means two devices. With 3 or more devices you get a multi-device discount.

Can I pay once instead of every month?

Yes. The lifetime licence is a one-time payment per device with one year of updates. After that you can keep using your version forever or add an update plan.

Does it work for restaurants and cafes?

Yes. Turn on the restaurant features for table maps, modifiers, a waiter app on phones, kitchen tickets and a kitchen screen, QR ordering by guests, split bills and a customer display. It all works on shop Wi-Fi without internet through the shop hub.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets, Windows and Linux PCs, iPhone and iPad, and the admin panel in any modern browser.

Which printers, scanners and scales work?

ESC/POS thermal receipt printers (USB, network, Bluetooth), cash drawers on the printer, USB/Bluetooth/serial barcode scanners and the phone camera, common weighing scales and scale barcodes. The shop guide has the full setup.

Can several tills share one catalog without internet?

Yes. Turn one PC or phone into the shop hub. The other tills find it over shop Wi-Fi or a USB link and stay in sync with no internet at all.

Do upcoming features cost extra?

No. Restaurant, kiosk and every other roadmap feature come as updates on an active plan.

Is card data safe?

The POS never reads or stores card numbers. Card terminals handle the payment and only send back "approved" or "declined".

Is GST included in the price?

Prices shown are before GST. GST is added on the invoice.

Policies

Privacy, terms and refunds

Privacy policy
  • Your shop data stays yours. Sales, products, customers and stock are stored on your devices. If you turn on sync, they are also kept on our cloud server. We don't sell, share or mine your data.
  • No ads, no tracking. The app has no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking SDKs, and doesn't need Google Play Services or Firebase. This website sets no cookies; it only remembers your light or dark choice in your browser.
  • Permissions are used only for selling. Camera: barcode scanning. Bluetooth, USB and serial: printers, scanners, scales and card terminals. Local network: finding the shop hub and other tills.
  • Card payments. The app never reads or stores card numbers. Card terminals only return approved or declined.
  • What we keep when you buy. Your name, shop name, phone or e-mail and GSTIN, to issue invoices and licences. We keep these as long as the law requires for invoices.
  • Deleting data. Uninstalling removes the data on that device. To have data on our cloud server or your contact details deleted, contact us.
